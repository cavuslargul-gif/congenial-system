# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads set and guide the engineering direction for a project. They ensure the solution architecture, technical standards, and implementation approach support the product goals while remaining sustainable for the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture decisions for the delivery team
- Review design trade-offs, technical risks, and dependencies with product and project leads
- Support engineers in solving complex technical issues and maintaining implementation quality
- Align engineering decisions with delivery timelines, reliability needs, and scalability requirements
- Partner with Project Managers to identify and resolve technical blockers

### Goals
- Deliver a robust, maintainable technical solution
- Reduce avoidable rework and delivery risk
- Keep engineering execution aligned with product priorities and standards

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Cross-team dependency planning and escalation updates
- Weekly alignment with PMs, Product Managers, and senior stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works closely with Developers to shape implementation approaches and technical quality expectations.
- Partners with Product Managers to translate roadmap priorities into feasible engineering plans.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on dependencies, sequencing, and risk mitigation for delivery.

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality strategy and ensure delivery meets user needs, acceptance criteria, and release readiness standards. They coordinate testing activities and help the team make confident go/no-go decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Review acceptance criteria and convert them into test coverage plans
- Coordinate defect triage, regression planning, and validation activities
- Partner with Developers on testability, automation, and validation quality
- Communicate quality confidence and risk to stakeholders and decision-makers

### Goals
- Improve product confidence and reduce defect escape risk
- Ensure quality expectations are clear and measurable
- Support smooth and predictable releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect triage, and release readiness reviews
- Sprint and release quality checkpoints
- Feedback loops with Product Managers, Developers, and PMs

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Developers to validate implementation quality and identify issues early.
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and customer-facing outcomes.
- Supports Project Managers by providing quality signals for milestone and release decisions.

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the project from technical, operational, and regulatory risk by ensuring security considerations are included early and continuously throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review product and technical designs for security, privacy, and compliance risks
- Define security requirements, control checks, and validation thresholds
- Partner with Engineering to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities and data-handling issues
- Coordinate required review gates, audits, and regulated-process checkpoints
- Escalate risks to PMs, Sponsors, and appropriate stakeholders when needed

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and compliance issues
- Build trust with users, stakeholders, and regulators
- Ensure risk controls are considered from planning through release

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, risk assessments, and compliance checkpoints
- Escalation updates for material issues or incidents
- Coordination with engineering, PM, and leadership stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to embed security practices into implementation and architecture.
- Provides Project Managers with risk and mitigation information needed for planning and escalation.
- Advises Product Managers and Sponsors on trade-offs involving policy, risk, and delivery constraints.

---

## Customer Success / Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Adoption Leads connect delivery work to customer outcomes, onboarding needs, and feedback loops. They help translate real-world usage and customer needs into product and release priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor customer needs, feedback, and adoption signals across the lifecycle
- Coordinate readiness for onboarding, rollout, and support handoff
- Translate user feedback into product and delivery priorities
- Partner with Product Managers and PMs to align release plans with adoption goals
- Support stakeholder communication about customer impact and readiness

### Goals
- Increase customer value realization and successful adoption
- Close the gap between product delivery and measurable customer outcomes
- Ensure customer context is represented in planning and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and adoption readiness updates
- Release communications and launch readiness coordination
- Collaboration with Product, PM, Support, and stakeholder teams

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Feeds customer insights into Product Manager prioritization and roadmap decisions.
- Works with Project Managers to align launch timing, communications, and support readiness.
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by clarifying user needs and practical usage scenarios.

---

## Executive Sponsor / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, funding alignment, and high-level decision support. They ensure the project remains aligned with business priorities and has the support needed to remove barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the initiative and maintain alignment with broader business objectives
- Support prioritization, funding, and cross-functional decision-making
- Help resolve escalated barriers, dependency conflicts, or strategic trade-offs
- Review milestones, benefits realization, and stakeholder alignment
- Provide executive messaging and support for major milestones or changes in scope

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers strategic value and business impact
- Maintain executive alignment and momentum for successful delivery
- Remove organizational obstacles that block project progress

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates, decision checkpoints, and milestone reviews
- Escalations for major risk, dependency, or business-impact issues
- Sponsorship and governance discussions with PM and Product leadership

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers on priority, trade-offs, and executive alignment.
- Supports Technical and Security Leads by providing escalation authority when cross-functional issues require leadership intervention.
- Works with Customer Success and stakeholder teams to ensure business value is understood and realized.

---

## How these roles work together

These personas complement the existing OctoAcme roles by clarifying accountability across the full project lifecycle. Product Managers define outcomes, Project Managers coordinate execution, Developers and Technical Leads build the solution, QA and Security Leads protect quality and risk, Customer Success connects delivery to customer impact, and Executive Sponsors ensure strategic support and decision authority.

Together, these roles create a clearer model for cross-functional collaboration, decision ownership, and accountability across planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.
